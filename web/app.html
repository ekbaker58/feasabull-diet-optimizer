Your week and shopping list

Choose how many days to eat each option. The week's food is X·d (grams per option times days per option). Packaged foods round up to whole packages; produce and meat sold by the pound are bought by weight.

Show the math

Targets: IOM Estimated Energy Requirement and Dietary Reference Intakes, the same method as FeasaBULL Calories. Nutrients: USDA SR28. Prices: Walmart (Oct 2026, regular prices) for the 71 core foods; BLS U.S. city averages (Sep 2026) or USDA ERS 2023 averages scaled to Sep 2026 with the BLS Consumer Price Index for the 75 added foods. U.S. averages are estimates, not a store's shelf price.

Limits keep plans realistic: no single food supplies more than 35% of the protein, every main meal carries at least 20% of the protein, and meat, beans, cheese, grains, oils and snacks are capped as groups. These are design choices by the team, not medical rules.

Educational estimates for healthy people 14 and older, not medical advice. Allergy filters cannot see a product's label. Unofficial student project, EGN4450 Team 9.
