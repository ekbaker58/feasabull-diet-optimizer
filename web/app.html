Targets: IOM Estimated Energy Requirement and Dietary Reference Intakes, the same method as FeasaBULL Calories. Nutrients: USDA SR28. Prices: Walmart, Oct 2026 (regular prices), or BLS U.S. city averages.

The plan is the cheapest combination of 71 foods that meets every target. Limits keep it realistic: no single food supplies more than 35% of the protein, every main meal carries at least 20% of the protein, and meat, beans, cheese, grains, oils and snacks are capped as groups.

Educational estimates for healthy people 14 and older, not medical advice. Unofficial student project, EGN4450 Team 9.
